She found it in the lamp room, under the lens, where the heat would have reached it. The pages had curled like *dry leaves*, and the last entry was in a hand she didn't know.

"You were never meant to read this," it began.

* * *

She read it anyway, all of it, standing, while the light turned over her head and the sea went on — patient, enormous, *indifferent* — below.
