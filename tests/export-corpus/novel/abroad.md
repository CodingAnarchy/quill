The first letter was in French: « Je reviendrai avant l'hiver, quoi qu'il arrive. » The second, from Thessaloniki, quoted a line her father had underlined twice: Θάλασσα, θάλασσα.

The last had come from Kobe, and was almost all in Japanese. She could read only the date and one word, written large: 灯台, lighthouse.

*Ça ira*, he'd written at the bottom, in pencil. It will be all right.
