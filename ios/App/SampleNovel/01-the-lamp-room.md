summary: Mara climbs the tower on the night her father doesn't come home.

The stairs had one hundred and twelve steps, and Mara knew which of them complained.

She took them two at a time anyway, because the light had gone out at a quarter past nine and her father had not come up to relight it. The tower smelled of paraffin and cold iron. Somewhere below, the sea was working at the rocks with the patience of something that had all night.

In the lamp room she found the matches where he always left them, in the tin with the picture of a steamer on the lid. Her hands were steady. That surprised her.

*He'll be at the Anchor,* she told herself. *He'll be telling the same story about the whale.*

The wick caught. The great lens turned, slow as a thought, and threw its beam out across the water, and in its sweep she saw a boat that should not have been there.
